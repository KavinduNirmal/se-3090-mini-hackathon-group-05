## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies communal stewardship, dignified warmth, and uncompromising logistical efficiency. It serves two distinct archetypes: high-volume hospitality donors (luxury Colombo hotels, artisanal bakeries, banquet operators) requiring swift, compliant operational dispatch, and community caretakers (orphanages, elder care facilities, non-profit distribution hubs) needing clear, dependable access to safe sustenance. 

The aesthetic is Modern Organic Functionalism—grounded, warm, and highly structured. It distances itself from clinical logistics software by honoring Sri Lankan agricultural vitality through lush foliage greens, sun-baked clay, and golden turmeric tones, while avoiding superficial decorative kitsch. Visual hierarchy is sharp, utilitarian, and deeply empathetic. The emotional response must be an immediate sense of shared abundance, operational trust, and mutual dignity.

Interfaces prioritize high-contrast clarity, legible dietary demarcation, verifiable hygiene checkpoints, and transparent, real-time rescue tracking.

## Colors

The color system operates on an ecological-to-culinary chromatic scale, anchored by rich chlorophyll greens and tempered with dry spice tones.

- **Primary (`#15803d` / Deep Emerald):** Represents agrarian vitality, growth, and trust. Used for primary interactive triggers, primary confirmation states, verified donor credentials, and central brand emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#d97706` / Spiced Amber):** Evokes raw turmeric, clay stoves, and urgency. Serves as the functional accent for time-critical food claims (e.g., "Collect within 90 mins"), pending acceptance badges, and secondary call-to-action touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#84cc16` / Tender Leaf Lime):** Deployed selectively for metric surges, real-time dispatch progress, freshness tags, and carbon offset milestones.
- **Neutral (`#1e293b` / Deep Slate Charcoal):** Provides high-contrast legibility for microcopy and critical nutritional details. Replaces harsh true-blacks with a warm, stony dark slate.

### Functional Surfaces & Backgrounds
- Base Canvas: `#f8fafc` (Warm Slate Crisp Off-White)
- Card Surfaces: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- Inset Containers / Subtle Wells: `#f1f5f9` (Cool Mist Slate)
- Borders & Dividers: `#e2e8f0` (Subtle Mineral Stroke)

### Dietary Badges & Indicators
- **Halal:** `#047857` (Cypress Jade) fill at 10% opacity with `#065f46` solid text and border.
- **Pure Veg:** `#15803d` (Emerald Leaf) bordered frame containing a solid green dot indicator.
- **Non-Veg:** `#b91c1c` (Deep Cinnamon Red) bordered frame containing a solid red triangle indicator.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural balance: warm, geometric humanist curves that stay open and legible under harsh sunlight on budget mobile devices, paired with professional crispness for desktop logistics desks.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headline Levels:** Use tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavier weights (700/800) to command attention and instill trust across meal listings and impact numbers.
- **Body Text:** Maintained strictly between 400 (Regular) and 500 (Medium) weights with open line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to prevent eye fatigue during rapid inventory audits and manifest reviews.
- **Data & Metric Numerals:** Numeric values in metrics, portions count, and pickup timers utilize tabular numeral features (`tnum`) to maintain vertical scanning harmony in tables and live feed cards.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Set in uppercase or capitalized medium weights with slightly expanded tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to ensure instant recognition of dietary constraints and food safety status.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px base rhythm, favoring an adaptive fluid grid system that flexes between busy kitchen prep stations (mobile web) and non-profit dispatch coordination desks (wide multi-column monitors).

### Breakpoints & Layout Architecture
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer margin. Cards stack vertically in single columns to maximize thumb tap targets and prevent accidental mis-claims during high-rush rescue windows.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Allows two-column food package tiles and split view panels for claim verification.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column fluid grid up to a maximum constrained width of `1280px` with 24px gutters and 32px margins. Accommodates multi-column operations: donor manifest lists, real-time routing maps, and telemetry analytics side-by-side.

All layout containers align strictly to internal padding scales: compact modules (metadata boxes, dietary tags) use `0.5rem` to `0.75rem`, while core structural modules (rescue cards, stats matrices) utilize `1.5rem` to `2rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic dropshadows and dense blurs, relying instead on clean, tactile layered surfaces and organic ambient lighting. Warm sunlight cast over terracotta inspires the shadow tinting: instead of neutral black shades, shadows contain a faint touch of deep slate and olive.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Flat / Ground Tier (`elevation-0`):** `#f8fafc` background with a subtle border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). Used for inactive cards, metric background blocks, and secondary inputs.
- **Card Surface (`elevation-1`):** Pure `#ffffff` surface, bounded by a fine stroke (`1px solid #e2e8f0`), casting a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for available food items, charity requests, and donor profiles.
- **Interactive Hover & Drag (`elevation-2`):** Elevates on mouseover or active touch: `0 8px 16px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 1px border shift to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Modals & Critical Sheets (`elevation-3`):** High-priority alerts, pickup verification QR modals, and claim approvals: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

Scrim overlays use `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)` with a delicate backdrop filter of `blur(4px)` to maintain context while focusing attention on critical transaction steps.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level `2` (medium-rounded: base `0.5rem` / `8px`). This structure balances institutional hygiene with human friendliness. 

### Corner Radii Conventions
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 8px):** Standard inputs, small utility badges, list item dividers, and action buttons.
- **Cards & Data Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Primary meal packages, inventory cards, charity profile cards, and impact graphs.
- **Structural Overlays & Drawers (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Top-level modal sheets, pickup confirmation dialogues, and bottom-sheet surfaces on mobile.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full`):** Dietary badges (Halal, Veg, Non-Veg), status markers (Active, Claimed, Picked Up), and portion counters.

- **Ghost/Outline Button:** White background, 1px border `#cbd5e1`, text `#1e293b`. Hover background `#f1f5f9`.

### Trust Badges & Verified Badges
- Verified donor credentials (e.g., "5-Star Food Hygiene Certified", "Direct Bakery Dispatch") utilize `#166534` fill, white shield/check icon, and paired slate metadata typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** 1px `#cbd5e1` outline on white, 8px radius, 12px horizontal padding. Focus state transitions to `#15803d` with an ambient glow. Micro-labels sit strictly above the input with a 4px gap.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px dimensions, 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Active state is solid `#15803d` with high-contrast white glyphs.

### Meal & Surplus Listing Cards
- Built using `#ffffff` surface, 16px radius, `elevation-1`, and 16px internal padding.
- Card header features donor name alongside an instant distance badge (`e.g., "1.4 km • Kollupitiya"`).
- Card body features bold quantity summaries (e.g., `"45 Portions • Prepared Rice & Curry"`), pickup countdown clock styled with `#d97706` background tint, and dietary badges aligned in a horizontal wrap.
- Card footer contains direct action trigger (`"Claim for Shelter"`) alongside a verified temperature/safe handling indicator.

### Impact Metric Modules
- Inset cards on `#f1f5f9` with an emerald top-accent line (`3px solid #15803d`).
- Prominently displays large tabular figures (`display-hero-mobile`) in deep slate, accompanied by tertiary leaf lime accents (`#84cc16`) for metrics like "Meals Saved" and "CO₂e Prevented".
